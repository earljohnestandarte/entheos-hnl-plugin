HNL Permit Pre-Check helps designers prepare residential permit plan sets for Honolulu’s CivCheck review. The web app analyzes PDFs in the browser, flags naming, sheet-format, and wording problems, finds the values CivCheck asks you to highlight, and exports a CivCheck-ready package. A companion browser extension guides you through the CivCheck session. A passing pre-check does not guarantee permit approval. This is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the City and County of Honolulu or Clariti.
