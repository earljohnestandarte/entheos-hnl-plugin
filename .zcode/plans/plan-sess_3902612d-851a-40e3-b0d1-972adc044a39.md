## Context
The UI currently feels like a generic product landing page: oversized slogans, repeated numbered stories, rounded cards inside cards, decorative symbols, badges, and a prominent dark summary panel. This is a document-preparation tool, so it should feel precise, quiet, and workmanlike instead.

I’ll apply an Impeccable-inspired design pass—clear hierarchy, purposeful contrast, restrained surfaces, and direct language. An Impeccable skill is not installed in this session; no third-party skill installation is needed for this change.

## Recommended design
**Direction: architectural review desk.** Warm off-white canvas, ink-colored text, subtle rules, one muted green action color, small corner radii, and tabular numbers for file metadata. No gradients, decorative icon tiles, marketing hero, or oversized success symbols.

### 1. Replace the landing-page treatment
- Remove the “Less backtracking. More prepared.” hero and repeated three-part marketing story from `components/Onboarding.tsx`.
- Present a compact setup document: “New preparation set,” a short functional introduction, progress labels, and the existing onboarding form.
- Retain the sample/test-PDF choice, name validation, session-limit acknowledgment, back navigation, and duplicate-start protection.
- Use a plain scope table/list rather than paired feature cards. Keep warnings legible without repeating them in every section.

### 2. Make the workspace utility-first
- Replace promotional headings in `App.tsx` with “Files,” “Findings,” and “CivCheck handoff.”
- Simplify the brand/header and step navigation; remove ornamental arrows, decorative number circles, excess badges, and redundant subtitles.
- Turn `PreparationSummary.tsx` into a quiet review ledger with aligned counts and coverage notes, not a dark feature card.
- Use borders, whitespace, and typographic weight to organize file rows and findings. Preserve visible categories and useful original/export filename distinctions.
- Make the guided sample a compact task list. Keep manual review separate from completed file fixes.
- Simplify handoff packaging into two clear sections: upload candidates and applicant references.

### 3. Consolidate visual styles
Rewrite the presentation rules in `apps/web/src/styles.css` around a small token set: type scale, spacing, ink/muted colors, borders, and focus states. Reduce nested containers, unnecessary shadows, pill treatments, and all-caps microcopy. Keep body text readable, touch targets practical, mobile reflow clean, and reduced-motion support intact.

Representative component edits: `Onboarding.tsx`, `StepNavigation.tsx`, `PreparationSummary.tsx`, `FilesPanel.tsx`, `FindingsPanel.tsx`, `HandoffPanel.tsx`, and `HelpDialog.tsx`.
